01 — The idea
A carousel is a short publication. Treat every template like a folio — numbered, sequenced, captioned.

The current build already believes the right things: the card is the template's shape, slides sit flush so a panorama reads as one image, previews render the real document. The redesign keeps those truths and moves the chrome from a dark tool palette to the warm stock of a printed magazine.

Identity comes from composition, type and photography — not from containers. There are no cards, no pills, no shadows. Paper tone and hairline rules do the separating.
